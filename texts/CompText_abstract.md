
## Heading:
# AutoConcourse: A Computational Pipeline for Automatic Generation of Q-Concourse Statements

## Option 1 (Me):
Q-methodology is a powerful mixed-method approach at the intersection of qualitative and quantitative research, enabling researchers to systematically study subjective viewpoints while retaining interpretability and statistical structure. Its validity, however, depends critically on the quality of the concourse: a collection of statements that meaningfully represents the diversity of public discourse around an issue. In practice, compiling and refining such a concourse remains labor-intensive, costly, and difficult to standardize across topics, languages, and research teams, with substantial challenges in ensuring coverage, diversity, and balance. We present AutoConcourse, a generalizable and steerable end-to-end pipeline for the automatic generation of Q-concourse statements from large public discourse corpora (e.g., newspaper articles and other issue-specific text sources). It employs sentence embeddings to map the discourse space and combines both supervised and unsupervised approaches to structure the corpus and extract relevant and diverse texts from the corpus. Finally, it leverages LLMs to synthesize these inputs into Q-concourse statements. For validation we compare AutoConourse’s outputs to human made Q-concourses. Importantly, the system is intended to assist and facilitate rather than replace expert judgement in final statement selection. To support responsible use, we provide quantitative diagnostics tailored to concourse construction tracking metrics such as redundancy, balance, and corpus coverage. With AutoConcourse, we aim to enable faster, more standardized concourse generation while retaining auditability and expert control over the final statement set.
